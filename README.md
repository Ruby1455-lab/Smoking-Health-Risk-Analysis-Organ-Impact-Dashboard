# Smoking-Health-Risk-Analysis-Organ-Impact-Dashboard

Project: Smoking Health Risk Analysis & Organ Impact Dashboard
Business Problem: Built an interactive healthcare analytics dashboard to evaluate the multi-system health impacts of smoking, tracking patient risk profiles across vital organs (Heart, Liver, Lungs, Kidney) and demographic variables.

Tools Used: Power BI Desktop, Advanced Data Modeling, Custom Navigation Panes, DAX Measures, Donut & Stacked Column Charts.

Key Metrics & Insights:
* Analyzed a cohort of 310 total patients, tracking a population average BMI of 28.8 and an average age of 47.6.
  
* Visualized smoking status distribution split evenly across segments (e.g., 40% Never, 30% Current, 30% Former).
  
* Incorporated multi-organ navigation panels (Human Body, Heart, Liver, Lungs, Kidney) to seamlessly filter risk categories (High, Low, Normal) and behavioral patterns across specific age and gender cohorts.
